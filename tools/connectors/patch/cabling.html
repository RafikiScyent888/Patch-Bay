A+ and Network+ both expect you to recognize connectors by their physical traits, not just their name. Every connector here is a 3D model drawn to its real dimensions, shown two ways: the end you plug in, and from above. Learn them in the gallery, then prove it in the drill.

Connector Gallery

End view: the face you push into the port. Top view: lying flat, the plug's nose pointing right. Ports are shown face-on and at an angle.

Drill — Identify the Connector

Six choices, one right. A wrong pick turns red and stays red so you can see what you've ruled out. Unlimited tries: after three misses you get a hint, and the hints keep coming. They never give you the answer.
